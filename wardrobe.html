How the rail works

Hang it, turn it, take it home.

01

Turn a piece

Everything hangs edge-on, the way it does in the shop. Run your cursor across the rail and the piece you want swings round to face you.

02

Open it

Click to pull it off the rail. The rest settle back along the bar. Pick your size, check what is left, and put it straight in the bag.

03

Set the hang

Puff changes how much of the garment you see. Flow lets the fabric move. Drop a transparent mockup on an open piece to reskin it.
